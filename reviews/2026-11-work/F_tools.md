## Decisions

1. **Tutum: Done.** Row removed from threat-modeling-tools.md. The domain has expired and serves a registrar parking page, and a link to a re-registrable domain is a safety risk. No official product page exists to replace it.
2. **Firmwalker: Done.** Row removed from firmware-and-binary-analysis.md. The official repository is gone. A fork would not be the product's official page, and choosing one would mean endorsing an unofficial maintainer. Binwalk, FACT, and the other tools still cover filesystem inspection.
3. **Pelion: Done.** Row removed from device-lifecycle-management.md. The cited site no longer offers device lifecycle management, so the description was false. Substituting Izuma Networks: Held back, because its only evidence is third-party press and izumanetworks.com did not resolve, so the evidence standard (an official source) cannot be met in this run. Eight other platforms remain on the page.
